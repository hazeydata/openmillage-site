Rusk County TX property tax rate, 2021 to 2025

2025 is preliminary (Texas Comptroller Working version). A Working or Final label on the source does not clear this flag.

Rusk County’s county total property tax rate is 0.544476 per $100 of taxable value in 2025, down from 0.631876 in 2021 (−0.087400). Cities add their own rate on top for property inside their limits.

About the unit. Every rate here is dollars per $100 of taxable value, so 0.5000 means 50 cents per $100. Texas Tax Code states that unit: “dollars per $100 of taxable value” (Tex. Tax Code §26.04(c)), “Total tax rate (per $100 of value)” (§26.062(d)(1)), and for non-ISD bodies “per $100 valuation” (§26.07). Unit basis: statutory_per_100.
