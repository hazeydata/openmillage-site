Florida independent special districts, 2025

Independent special districts are local taxing bodies with their own boards, such as water management, fire, hospital and children's services districts. Each sets its own millage (dollars per $1,000 of taxable value), and it applies only to property inside the district. Pick a district to see its 2025 rate in every county where we have a sourced row.
