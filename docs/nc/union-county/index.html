Union County NC property tax rate, FY2021-22 to FY2025-26

Union County's general property tax rate is 0.4342 per $100 of assessed value in FY2025-26, down from 0.5880 in FY2021-22 (-0.1538). Cities and towns add their own rate on top for property inside their limits.

About the unit. Every rate here is dollars per $100 of assessed value, so 0.5000 means 50 cents per $100. Only North Carolina DOR's FY2025-26 county rate PDF states that unit (“(All rates per $100 valuation*)”, source). The DOR spreadsheets the other rates come from carry no unit label, so for those rates the unit is inferred, not stated. In DOR's words from our data notes: “NC DOR doesn't state a unit for this rate; we read it as per $100 based on older DOR tables” (Table 67). Rates marked i use the inferred unit.
